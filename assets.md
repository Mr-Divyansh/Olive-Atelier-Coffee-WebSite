## Candidate discovery providers

The brief names [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/) and [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/) as possible discovery sources. These are provider homepages supplied by the brief, **not verified individual image URLs**, and no particular asset or license has been checked. Review current provider terms, image-specific conditions, attribution expectations, and hotlinking/download rules at selection time. Do not scrape or bypass access restrictions.

## Verification and implementation policy

Before an asset is used, record its exact stable source URL, creator/provider, date checked, applicable license/attribution, downloaded local path (if any), intended use, and verification outcome. Confirm the URL responds and that the actual image suits the content. Prefer appropriately sized local optimized images where practical and permitted; otherwise use a stable documented source mechanism. Do not fabricate source URLs or claim assets were downloaded. Supply context-appropriate alt text and record decorative images as decorative.

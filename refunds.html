Refunds & cancellations

Concept draft. Last updated for review purposes only.

Draft template, not legal advice. This page is a starting point for the Olive Atelier concept. Because no payments are currently taken, no refund policy is in force. The points below must be reviewed by a qualified legal advisor and agreed with the real business before launch.

No payments are taken today

This website does not process payments, take deposits, or sell anything. The booking and contact forms are demonstrations only and do not create a reservation or charge of any kind.

If payments are introduced later

Before accepting deposits or selling gift cards, vouchers, or experiences, a real policy will be published here. It should clearly cover, at minimum:

  • Notice periods for cancelling or changing a reservation, and any no-show terms.
  • Whether deposits are refundable, and the process and timeframe for refunds.
  • How refunds are returned (original payment method) and who to contact.
  • Consumer rights that apply under relevant law, which cannot be waived by this policy.

Where to ask

For now, any questions can be raised through the contact page. A dedicated contact for refunds and cancellations will be confirmed before any payment feature goes live.

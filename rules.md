# Project Rules

- Preserve working functionality and inspect the repository before proposing structural changes.
- Treat the Olive Atelier palette in `design.md` as the visual source of truth.
- Do not add dependencies or integrations without a concrete need.
- Never fabricate business facts, reviews, prices, awards, opening hours, contact information, or statistics.
- Clearly identify demo placeholders and replace them with approved content before production.
- Verify image sources, use rights, attribution requirements, and actual files; do not claim unverified assets are ready.
- Do not present stock photography as the actual restaurant or its guests.
- Prioritize keyboard accessibility, readable contrast, semantic structure, reduced motion, responsive behavior, and performance.
- Avoid generic AI-template aesthetics, excessive gradients/effects, random palette additions, and needless animation.
- Prefer maintainable, reusable components without overengineering.
- Never expose API keys, credentials, or other secrets.
- Do not collect personal data or claim legal compliance without a defined, reviewed implementation.
- Test actual behavior and report only checks that were actually performed.
- Follow the phase gates in `phases.md`; Phase 1 is documentation only.
